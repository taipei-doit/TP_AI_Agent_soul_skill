# Sources of laws and guidelines

Translations of laws and guidelines are unofficial; the Chinese original prevails.

The original sources of all laws and guidelines cited in these guidelines and in soul.md are listed here. Laws change, so before citing one formally, check the latest version at the source URL.

Markers: [Law] laws or self-government ordinances; [Gov] guidelines, regulations, or rules issued by the Executive Yuan, central ministries, or the Taipei City Government.

Date checked: the contents of these tables were checked against the original texts at the sources below on October 12, 2026 (ROC year 115).
